## ✅ 5‑Question Quiz (with Answers)

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | **Which two public repositories are most commonly used for obtaining exoplanet data in this workflow?** | NASA Exoplanet Archive and Kepler DR25 (Data Release 25). |
| 2 | **Name one advantage of using SHAP over simple feature‑importance scores when interpreting a tree‑based model.** | SHAP provides **local** explanations (per‑instance contribution) and satisfies properties of additivity and consistency, whereas traditional importance scores are only **global**. |
| 3 | **In the DiCE framework, what does the term “diverse” refer to?** | It means generating multiple counterfactuals that are **different from each other** (varying feature changes) while still achieving the desired target prediction. |
| 4 | **Give one astrophysical constraint that should be enforced when filtering counterfactual exoplanet candidates.** | Example: The orbital period and semi‑major axis must obey **Kepler’s third law** ( \(P^2 \propto a^3\) ), or the planet radius cannot exceed the host star’s radius. |
| 5 | **List two evaluation metrics used to compare ordinary and physically constrained counterfactual explanations.** | Validity (does the counterfactual achieve the target class?) and Physical Plausibility (does it respect astrophysical constraints?). Other acceptable answers: Proximity, Sparsity, Stability. |
